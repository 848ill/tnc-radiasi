# Surface Brief: TNC Home & Live Session Hub (`web/src/app/page.tsx`)

- **Scope:** Primary landing page, live Open Play & coaching session booking console, tournament registration hub, and club membership showcase (`/`).
- **Visitor Mode:** Persuade (with immediate Operate booking utility in the right-hand console).
- **Approved Comp:** `.impeccable/mocks/comp-3.png` (carrying forward Comp 2's quick session filter bar and 3-column feature cards into the below-the-fold sections).

## Audience & Task
- **Audience:** Urban tennis players and TNC members in Jakarta checking weekly Open Play drops, tournament slots, and membership tiers.
- **Primary Action:** Select an active Open Play / Tournament / Coaching session, inspect remaining slot capacity, preview the QR Court Pass, and lock the slot via QRIS (`BOOK SLOT & PAY QRIS`).
